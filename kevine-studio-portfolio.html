Kigali, Rwanda · Multidisciplinary Artist

Kevine NI is a contemporary artist whose practice moves fluidly across painting, sculpture, ceramics, and pyrography — each discipline an extension of a singular creative language rooted in Rwandan cultural memory.

Working from her studio in Kigali, Kevine draws on the geometric vocabulary of Imigongo — the ancient Rwandan art form — reimagining its patterns through a contemporary lens. The work interrogates identity, material memory, and the tension between tradition and modernity.

Her practice has been exhibited across East Africa and internationally, and she maintains an active programme of private commissions, workshops, and collector residencies.
